# Sleep and stress

Most adults need 7 to 9 hours of sleep. Good sleep habits: fixed sleep and wake times, no caffeine after mid-afternoon, a dark cool bedroom, no screens for an hour before bed, and daily light exercise but not right before sleep.

Stress management: slow breathing (inhale for 4 seconds, exhale for 6), a short daily walk, talking to someone you trust, limiting news and social media, and breaking tasks into small steps.

See a doctor or counsellor if low mood, anxiety or poor sleep lasts more than two weeks or affects work and relationships. In India, Tele-MANAS (14416) offers free, confidential mental health support 24 hours a day. If you have thoughts of harming yourself, call 112 or 14416 right away.
