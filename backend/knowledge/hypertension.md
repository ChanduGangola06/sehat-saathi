# High blood pressure (hypertension)

A normal adult blood pressure is generally below 120/80 mmHg. Readings repeatedly at or above 130/80 mmHg are considered high by many guidelines, and 140/90 mmHg or above usually calls for medical evaluation. A diagnosis needs repeated readings, not a single one.

How to measure at home: sit quietly for five minutes, feet flat, back supported, arm at heart level, no caffeine or smoking for 30 minutes before. Take two readings a minute apart and record them.

Lifestyle that lowers blood pressure: less salt (under about 5 g a day), more fruits, vegetables and whole grains, regular walking or exercise for about 150 minutes a week, healthy weight, limited alcohol, no tobacco, good sleep and stress management.

Never stop or change blood pressure medicines without asking your doctor. A reading of 180/120 mmHg or higher with chest pain, breathlessness, severe headache, weakness or vision changes is an emergency.
