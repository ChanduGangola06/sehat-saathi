# Headache: general guidance

Most headaches are tension-type or migraine and are not dangerous. Common triggers include dehydration, missed meals, poor sleep, screen strain, stress and caffeine changes.

Self-care: drink water, rest in a quiet dim room, eat regularly, reduce screen time, and gentle neck stretches.

Warning signs that need urgent care: a sudden, severe "worst headache of my life", headache after a head injury, with fever and stiff neck, with confusion, weakness, vision loss or trouble speaking, or a new headache after age 50.

See a doctor if headaches become more frequent, wake you from sleep, or need pain medicine more than two or three days a week.
