# Cold, cough and flu

Most colds and coughs are viral and improve in 7 to 10 days. Antibiotics do not work on viruses.

Self-care: rest, fluids, warm water and soups, honey in warm water for cough (not for children under 1 year), saline nasal rinse, steam inhalation with care to avoid burns, and avoiding smoke.

See a doctor if symptoms last more than 10 days, a fever lasts more than three days, there is wheezing, chest pain, or the person has asthma, heart or lung disease, diabetes, or is pregnant or elderly.

Seek urgent care for breathing difficulty, blue lips, coughing blood, or confusion.
