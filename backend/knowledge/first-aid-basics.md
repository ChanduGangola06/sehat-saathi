# First aid basics

Minor cuts: wash hands, rinse the wound with clean running water, press with a clean cloth to stop bleeding, then cover with a clean bandage. Seek care if the cut is deep, gaping, will not stop bleeding after 10 minutes of firm pressure, or is dirty and you may need a tetanus shot.

Minor burns: cool under cool running water for 20 minutes. Do not apply ice, butter or toothpaste. Do not pop blisters. Seek care for burns that are large, on the face, hands, genitals or joints, or are deep.

Sprains: rest, ice for 15 to 20 minutes at a time, light compression, and elevation. See a doctor if the person cannot bear weight or there is obvious deformity.

Emergencies: for chest pain, trouble breathing, stroke signs (face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty), unconsciousness, seizures, severe bleeding or suspected poisoning, call 112 or 108 in India right away.
