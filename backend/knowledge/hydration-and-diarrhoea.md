# Hydration and diarrhoea

Adults generally need around 2 to 3 litres of fluid a day, more in hot weather or during exercise. Pale yellow urine usually means good hydration.

For diarrhoea or vomiting, the main risk is dehydration. Use oral rehydration solution (ORS) in small, frequent sips, continue eating light foods like rice, banana, curd and khichdi, and avoid alcohol and very sugary drinks.

See a doctor if there is blood in the stool, diarrhoea lasts more than two days, there is high fever, or the person is elderly, pregnant or has a chronic illness.

Urgent care is needed for signs of severe dehydration: very little or no urine, extreme thirst, dizziness on standing, sunken eyes, or confusion. Babies and young children dehydrate quickly and should be seen early.
